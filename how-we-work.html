my process

From idea to impact, in six steps.

No black box. You know exactly what stage your product is at, every step of the way.

01

Discover

Understand your goals, your users, and the real problem worth solving — before any code gets written.

02

Plan

Design the right architecture — backend, data model, and platform choices — so the product scales without a rewrite later.

03

Build

Develop with best practices — clean commits, secure auth, and a shared backend powering both web and iOS.

04

Test

Multi-round manual testing across API, web, and device — bugs get caught before your users find them.

05

Launch

Deploy to production and, where relevant, ship to the App Store — with docs and a clean handoff.

06

Support

Stick around for fixes, iterations, and the next feature — not a one-and-done handoff.
